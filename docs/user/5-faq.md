# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do customers need an account?

No. Customers submit the public [TA Request Form](https://taconnect.lbl.gov/intake) and receive updates by email.

## How do National Laboratory users log in?

Click **ORCiD** on the login page and authenticate through ORCiD. See [Getting Started](getting-started.md).

## Why can I log in but not see any requests?

You may not have a role, or your role may not apply to any current requests. Open **Profile** and check **My System Roles** and **My Organizational Roles**. If you do not have a role, use the role request form linked on that page.

## Why is an edit or assignment button missing?

TA Connect displays actions based on your role, the request's current owner, and its status. For example, a Lab Lead cannot assign a request until it is assigned to that Lead's laboratory. See [Roles and Permissions](roles-and-permissions.md).

## What is the difference between Actionable and Downstream requests?

An **Actionable** request is currently at a level where one of your roles can act. A **Downstream** request has moved to a later level but remains visible for tracking.

## Where are completed requests?

Open **Requests** and select the **Inactive** tab. This tab also includes requests marked **Unable to Address**.

## Can I reopen a request?

An authorized Coordinator, Program Lead, or Admin can open an inactive request and click **Reopen request**. The exact action available depends on the request and role scope.

## How do I correct customer or organization information?

Open the request and use the edit control in the customer or organization panel. You can also open the **Customers** or **Organizations** page and use the edit icon. Some changes and all deletion actions require additional permissions.

## What happens if a customer or organization is a duplicate?

On a request, an authorized user can transfer the request to the correct existing customer or organization. Admins can then delete a duplicate where appropriate. Deleting a customer or organization removes it from all associated requests, so review the request counts and transfer affected requests first.

## Who can see notes and attachments?

Notes and attachments are part of the internal request record and are available to authorized TA Connect users who can view that request. Do not treat them as customer-facing messages. Customers receive separately generated email notifications.

## Can I delete a note or attachment?

Authorized users can delete notes and attachments from requests within their role scope. Deletion is permanent in the user interface, so confirm that you selected the correct item.

## How are closeout drafts saved?

While an editable closeout form is open, TA Connect saves changes shortly after you stop typing. It also saves an unsubmitted draft when the form closes. Click the submit action only when the closeout information is ready for review.

## How do I return a closeout form for more information?

During laboratory or program review, click **Reject and send back to expert**. The request returns to the Expert so the closeout responses can be updated and submitted again.

## Can I download data?

The Experts table can be printed or downloaded as CSV from its **Export** menu. Request-list export and a separate reporting dashboard are not currently available. See [Reports and Dashboards](reports-and-dashboards.md).

## I submitted an intake form but need a status update. Who should I contact?

Email `TA3@lbl.gov` about the status of an existing request or submission. Watch for automated request messages from `taconnect@lbl.gov`.

## What should I do if the intake form fails?

Check all required fields, confirm that the email and ten-digit US phone number are valid, and submit again. If the problem continues, contact `taconnect@lbl.gov`.
