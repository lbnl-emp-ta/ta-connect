# Getting Started

National Laboratory users sign in to TA Connect with ORCiD. Customers do not sign in. Instead they use the public intake form and receive automated updates via email.

## Log in

1. Go to [https://taconnect.lbl.gov](https://taconnect.lbl.gov).
2. Click **Continue with ORCiD** on the login page.
3. Log in to ORCiD and authorize access if ORCiD asks you to do so.
4. TA Connect returns you to the **Profile** page if this is your first login or the **Requests** page if you are already situated with your roles and user info.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** TA Connect login page with the ORCiD button highlighted.

If login fails, TA Connect displays the error returned by the identity provider. Click **Back to login** and try again. If the problem continues, contact the TA Connect team.

## Complete your profile

Open the menu with your name in the upper-right corner and select **Profile**. Your profile shows your contact information and all roles assigned to you.

If your email address is missing, or if your name or phone number is incorrect, click the edit icon next to your email address. Update the information and save it. A valid email address is important because TA Connect uses email for request notifications.

Also note that a valid U.S. phone number and email address are required to continue using TA Connect. If this information isn't saved in the system, you will be automatically prompted to provide it.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** Profile page with the contact information edit icon highlighted.

## Check your roles

Your profile separates roles into **My System Roles** and **My Organizational Roles**. System roles apply across TA Connect. Organizational roles apply to a particular program and laboratory.

If you do not have a role, TA Connect displays a link to the role request form. Submit that form and wait for approval. Your new role appears the next time TA Connect loads your profile after it has been assigned.

Experts should also review the areas of expertise shown with each Expert role. Click **Edit Expertise** to update the topic and depth combinations associated with that program and laboratory assignment.

See [Roles and Permissions](roles-and-permissions.md) for more information.

## Navigate TA Connect

The navigation bar contains these pages:

- **Requests** lists active and inactive requests that are available to your roles.
- **Experts** lists experts, their areas of expertise, and their request counts.
- **Customers** lists customer contacts and their request counts.
- **Organizations** lists customer organizations.
- **Intake** opens the public request form.

Use the menu with your name to return to **Profile** or to **Log out**.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** Main navigation bar and user menu.

## Admin Mode

Users with the Admin role see an **Admin Mode** switch on the Requests and Experts pages. Turn it on when you need system-wide visibility or administrator actions. When it is off, the pages use your other assigned roles and their normal scope.

## Log out

Open the menu with your name and select **Log out**. TA Connect returns you to the login page.
