# Submitting a Request

The TA Request Form is public. Customers do not need a TA Connect account to submit it.

Go to [https://taconnect.lbl.gov/intake](https://taconnect.lbl.gov/intake). The program is limited to state public utility commissions (PUCs) and state energy offices (SEOs). Contact `TA3@lbl.gov` for the status of an existing submission.

## Before you begin

Have the following information ready:

- Your name, email address, phone number, and job title
- Your organization name and address
- A description of the requested assistance
- The challenge or issue prompting the request
- The outcome you want to achieve
- Your best estimate of the hands-on effort required

Fields marked with an asterisk are required.

## Enter your contact and organization information

Enter your name, email, ten-digit US phone number, and job title. TA Connect sends request notifications to the email address you enter.

Select your organization from the list. If it is not listed, select **Other** and enter the organization name, address, type, state, and transmission planning region. TA Connect uses this information to create an organization record for the request.

## Describe the request

Complete these sections:

- **Request description:** Describe the technical assistance you are requesting.
- **Challenges:** Explain the challenge, issue, or context that led to the request.
- **Goals:** Describe the outcome you want from the assistance, such as a report, workshop, or support for a non-litigated proceeding.
- **Level of effort:** Select your best estimate of the total hands-on working time.

The description, challenges, and goals fields each accept up to 4,000 characters. Estimate work time rather than elapsed calendar time. One day of effort means eight working hours.

## Submit the form

Review the information and click **Submit** once. When the submission succeeds, the page displays **Form submitted!** and tells you to look for email from `taconnect@lbl.gov`. Use **Submit another TA request** only when you need to create a separate request.

If an error appears, check the required fields and your contact information, then try again. If the error continues, contact `taconnect@lbl.gov`.

## What happens next

TA Connect sends a confirmation email and places the request with the reception team. A Coordinator reviews the request and routes it to an appropriate program. Later notifications are sent as the request moves through the workflow.
